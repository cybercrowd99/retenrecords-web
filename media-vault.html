Reten Media Vault

Reten Media Vault is the future media inventory lane for Reten Records. It is where audio, video, covers, clips, teasers, session media, show media, previews, and catalog assets may be organized before they move into public releases, shows, records, or archive references.

Media Vault Purpose

The Media Vault is not just storage. It is a working inventory for creative media assets that may later become releases, live sessions, show segments, Record Room products, Archive witnesses, or catalog entries.

Future Media Types

  • Audio previews
  • Full-length audio masters
  • Video clips
  • Show segments
  • Live session recordings
  • Interview recordings
  • Cover art
  • Behind-the-scenes media
  • Teasers
  • Catalog images
  • Physical record artwork
  • Archive reference media

Vault to Archive

The Media Vault may hold working media assets. The Reten Archive preserves proof, version history, meaning, and custody. A media file may begin in the Vault, become a release, and later become an Archive witness.

Connection to Reten

Media Vault may feed Releases, Artists, Shows, Live Sessions, Record Room, Casting Calls, Collaborations, and the Reten Archive.
