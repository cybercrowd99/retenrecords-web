Reten Archive

The Reten Archive is the depository foundation for Reten Records. It exists to retain, record, preserve, and reference music, media, releases, sessions, shows, records, and creative artifacts.

Archive Purpose

Storage holds files. A depository holds meaning, custody, proof, version history, and relationship. The Reten Archive is designed to become the public doorway into Reten Records’ preservation layer.

What the Reten Archive May Hold

  • Release history
  • Artist references
  • Session notes
  • Interview records
  • Show references
  • Version history
  • Catalog artifacts
  • Physical record references
  • Flyers, images, covers, and media notes
  • Proof objects connected to Reten Records releases and events

The Record Is the Witness

Reten Records treats records, media artifacts, catalog entries, and preserved versions as witnesses. A record, pressing, session, photograph, flyer, or source note can anchor what existed, when it existed, and how it was preserved.

Reten and CyberCrowd Reciprocity

CyberCrowd is Reten Records’ sandbox, incubator, and testing world. Reten Records is the public music, records, media, show, release, and catalog vessel.

In the future, Reten and CyberCrowd may use reciprocal archive references. Reten can cite CyberCrowd when a sandbox experiment, tool, or live system shapes a Reten release or show. CyberCrowd can cite Reten when a media artifact, record, show, or catalog event proves a CyberCrowd concept.

Each archive keeps independent custody. Reciprocity means reference, not confusion.
