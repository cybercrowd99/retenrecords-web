About Reten Records

Reten Records is the public music, records, media, show, and catalog vessel for RetenRecords.com.

Reten Records exists to retain, record, preserve, and release music and media as more than disposable files. The long-term purpose includes releases, artist pages, auditions, interviews, live sessions, collaborations, physical records, shows, and future catalog access.

What Reten Records Holds

  • Music releases
  • Artist identities
  • Record and vinyl concepts
  • Auditions and casting opportunities
  • Interviews and live sessions
  • Collaborations
  • Shows such as Atomic Kricket
  • Media vault and catalog access

Relationship to CyberCrowd

CyberCrowd is Reten Records’ sandbox, incubator, and testing world. RetenRecords.com is the public-facing music, records, media, show, release, and catalog vessel.

The systems are connected by vision, but production wiring must be built carefully and intentionally.
