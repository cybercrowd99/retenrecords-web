Reten Casting Calls

Reten Casting Calls is the creative access board and asset discovery lane for Reten Records. It is where people, art, compositions, performers, stories, characters, sessions, and future catalog material can enter the Reten world.

This is not an employment page. It is not a standard audition page. It is not a linear social feed. Reten Casting Calls is closer to a creative bulletin board, talent directory, room system, casting desk, and asset intake portal.

Creative Asset Discovery

The asset side of Reten Records begins with discovery: artists, voices, performers, musicians, compositions, visual work, personalities, stories, and media moments that can become sessions, shows, releases, records, archive entries, collaborations, or catalog assets.

Portal Board Logic

The long-term model is closer to an old-school access board than a modern feed. Reten Casting Calls may support open calls, talent rooms, performer listings, session requests, show guest requests, saved candidates, recalled profiles, and review queues.

Future Review Workflow

A future Reten Casting Calls system may support grid-based review, with many casting cards visible at once. Each card may include an image, short profile, performance reference, composition reference, media sample, and casting interview.

  • Open a casting call
  • Review creative profile cards
  • Click an image or profile to view a short casting interview
  • Save promising candidates or assets
  • Deny entries that are not a fit
  • Review saved entries later
  • Recall candidates for sessions, shows, releases, records, or collaborations

Separate From Employment

Casting Calls are creative participation and asset discovery opportunities. Employment openings for Reten Records or CyberCrowd would be handled separately as formal job listings, not through this page.

Connection to the Reten System

Casting Calls may feed Artists, Releases, Shows, Live Sessions, Collaborations, Record Room, and the Reten Archive. A person, performance, composition, story, or artifact discovered here may later become part of the Reten catalog.
