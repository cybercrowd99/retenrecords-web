Reten Catalog

Reten Catalog is the future index for Reten Records releases, artists, records, shows, live sessions, media vault assets, archive entries, collaborations, and catalog access.

Catalog Purpose

The Catalog is where Reten Records can organize creative assets after they move through discovery, sessions, collaboration, release planning, media holding, physical production, or archive preservation.

Future Catalog Areas

  • Music releases
  • Artist profiles
  • Physical records
  • LPs and 45s
  • Live sessions
  • Show segments
  • Atomic Kricket entries
  • Media Vault assets
  • Archive references
  • Collaboration records
  • Catalog numbers
  • Version notes

Not a Linear Feed

Reten Records is not built as a linear feed. The Catalog may connect many spokes of the Reten system: Casting Calls, Artists, Releases, Shows, Live Sessions, Collaborations, Record Room, Media Vault, and Archive.

Connection to Reten

A catalog entry may become a release page, a record product, a show segment, an archive witness, a media vault asset, or a future access point.
