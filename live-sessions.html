Reten Live Sessions

Reten Live Sessions is the live media lane for Reten Records. It is where performances, interviews, creative rooms, session events, collaborations, and live media moments can enter the Reten system.

Live Session Purpose

Reten Live Sessions are not just streams. They are creative events that may connect artists, performers, speakers, collaborators, audiences, shows, records, archive entries, casting calls, and future catalog material.

Future Live Session Types

  • Artist performances
  • Studio sessions
  • Interview sessions
  • Collaboration sessions
  • Atomic Kricket segments
  • Record Room demonstrations
  • Archive proof reviews
  • Creative room events
  • Multi-platform live events
  • Audience discovery sessions

Rotating Media Logic

Reten Records is not built as a linear feed. A live session may rotate attention between an artist, a record, a story, a performance, an interview, an archive object, a casting call, a collaboration, or an audience moment.

Session Review and Recall

Future live sessions may connect to a review and recall workflow. A person, performance, conversation, composition, or media moment discovered in a live session may later be saved, reviewed, recalled, and connected to a release, show, collaboration, archive entry, or Record Room product.

Connection to Reten

Live Sessions may feed Artists, Releases, Casting Calls, Collaborations, Shows, Record Room, and the Reten Archive. A session is not just a broadcast; it can become part of the Reten catalog and preservation system.
