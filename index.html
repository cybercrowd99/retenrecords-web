Reten Records

Reten Records is the official music and media brand at RetenRecords.com. It is being built as a web and media foundation for independent releases, artist pages, auditions, interviews, live sessions, collaborations, previews, and future catalog access.

Reten Records Purpose

Reten Records is focused on music, media, release discovery, artist identity, live creative sessions, interviews, collaborations, and future full-length music access.

Brand Separation

RetenRecords.com is separate from CyberCrowd.net. Reten Records is the music, media, releases, auditions, interviews, live sessions, collaborations, artist pages, previews, and catalog brand. CyberCrowd remains the separate platform, WDIG, Magic Cursor, access pass, tools, device, and broader ecosystem project.

Current Status

Reten Records is in its foundation stage. The current page exists to establish the RetenRecords.com domain, public identity, search visibility, and basic crawler-readable structure before the full visual site and media systems are built.
