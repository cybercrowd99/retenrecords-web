Reten Collaborations

Reten Collaborations is the creative connection lane for Reten Records. It is where artists, musicians, performers, speakers, storytellers, producers, visual creators, guests, and media workers may connect around sessions, shows, releases, records, and shared creative work.

Collaboration Purpose

Collaboration is not just a feature. It is how creative assets can move from discovery into sessions, shows, releases, archive entries, Record Room products, and future catalog material.

Future Collaboration Types

  • Artist-to-artist collaborations
  • Musician and vocalist pairings
  • Producer and composer collaborations
  • Visual artist and mural concepts
  • Interview and show guest collaborations
  • Live session collaborations
  • Atomic Kricket segment collaborations
  • Record Room physical release collaborations
  • Archive proof and story collaborations
  • Cross-media creative projects

Rotating Platform Logic

Reten Records is not a linear feed. A collaboration may begin from a casting call, a live session, a show idea, a record, an archive object, a visual concept, a performance, or a story. Any spoke can become the center when the creative work rotates into view.

Connection to Reten

Collaborations may feed Artists, Releases, Casting Calls, Live Sessions, Shows, Record Room, and the Reten Archive. A collaboration can become a release, a show segment, a physical record, a preserved artifact, or a future catalog asset.
